# Content Research and Sourcing — STAY Properties

STAY Properties is a hypothetical South African small business created for this academic project. Original content was written for the organisation because it is fictional.

## Target audience
- First-time home buyers
- Families
- Young professionals
- Tenants
- Property owners seeking listing assistance

## Page content
- **Home:** introduction, value proposition, featured listings and calls to action.
- **About Us:** company story, mission/vision and team.
- **Properties:** residential listings, locations, descriptions and enquiry links.
- **Enquiry:** prospective-client requirements and contact details.
- **Contact:** two offices, contact details, map links and contact form.

## Research/sourcing approach
Business descriptions, team descriptions and property descriptions are original academic content. Images are stored locally for portability. Before public deployment, exact image licences/permissions should be confirmed and recorded in `image-source-log.md`.

## General technical sources
- W3C — web standards and accessibility.
- MDN — semantic HTML and form guidance.
- Google Maps — location links.
- PPRA — South African property-industry context.
